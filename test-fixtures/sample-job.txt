Senior Data Analyst – Supply Chain

We are looking for a Senior Data Analyst to join our supply chain analytics team. You will partner with stakeholders across operations and finance to build reporting, forecast demand and improve process efficiency.

What you'll do
- Design and maintain dashboards in Power BI and Tableau
- Write complex SQL queries against our Snowflake data warehouse
- Build demand forecasting models in Python
- Lead A/B testing and statistical analysis for operational changes
- Communicate insights to senior stakeholders

What we're looking for
- 4+ years of experience in data analysis, ideally in supply chain or logistics
- Strong SQL and Python skills; experience with dbt is a plus
- Experience with ETL pipelines and data modeling
- Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills
- Familiarity with Agile ways of working
